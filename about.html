Our Story

Why Poshana Exists

Poshana means nourishment. We started this clinic with a simple belief: good nutrition should feel personal, practical and achievable — not confusing or restrictive.

Too often, people are handed generic diet charts that ignore their routines, cultural foods and real-life challenges. At Poshana, every plan begins with listening. We take time to understand your health, your habits and your goals, then build guidance around the way you actually live.

The result is nutrition support that fits into your life — and lasts.

What Guides Us

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To make personalised, evidence-informed nutrition guidance accessible, practical and easy to follow for every individual and family we support.

Our Vision

A community where healthy eating is understood, enjoyed and sustained — helping people live fuller, more energetic lives.

Our Values

Empathy, honesty and respect for every client — paired with practical advice and a commitment to long-term wellbeing over quick fixes.

Our Approach

Thoughtful, Personal & Practical

We combine nutrition science with an understanding of everyday life. That means guidance that respects your food culture, your schedule and your budget.

  • Listen first. We understand your health history, lifestyle and preferences before recommending anything.
  • Keep it practical. Plans use familiar, easy-to-find foods and realistic meal routines.
  • Build habits gradually. Small, steady changes that are easier to keep up over time.
  • Stay alongside you. Regular follow-ups to review progress and adjust your plan.
  • Work with your care team. Nutrition guidance that complements the advice of your doctor.
