About Poshana

Nutrition That Understands You

Poshana is a nutrition and wellness clinic dedicated to personalised dietary guidance. We believe good nutrition should feel achievable — rooted in the foods you enjoy, shaped around your daily routine, and focused on changes you can sustain for life.

Whether you are managing a health condition, working towards a healthier weight or simply want to eat better, we help you build healthier habits one practical step at a time.

  • Personalised dietary guidance, not one-size-fits-all plans
  • Sustainable lifestyle improvements that last
  • Support for healthier living at every stage of life
