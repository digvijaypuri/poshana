Our Services

Personalised nutrition support for weight, medical conditions, hormonal health and every stage of family life.

01

Weight Management

Whether your goal is to lose, gain or maintain weight, we create a balanced plan that works with your appetite, routine and food preferences — no crash diets or extreme restrictions.

  • Detailed assessment of current eating habits
  • Portion guidance and practical meal structure
  • Strategies for cravings, eating out and travel
Book a Consultation
02

Clinical Nutrition

Nutrition guidance for people living with medical conditions, designed to complement the care and advice of their doctor.

  • Review of health history and reports you share
  • Condition-appropriate meal planning
  • Ongoing adjustments as your needs change
Book a Consultation
03

PCOS Nutrition

Food and lifestyle strategies to help support hormonal balance, steadier energy and overall wellbeing for women with PCOS.

  • Balanced meals to support steady energy
  • Guidance on activity, sleep and stress habits
  • Realistic, long-term weight support where needed
Book a Consultation
04

Diabetes Management

Practical meal planning to help manage blood sugar levels, alongside the treatment plan from your doctor.

  • Understanding carbohydrates and portion sizes
  • Meal timing that suits your routine
  • Smart choices for festivals, travel and eating out
Book a Consultation
05

Child Nutrition

Support for parents on balanced meals, healthy growth and building positive, lifelong eating habits — including for picky eaters.

  • Age-appropriate meal and snack ideas
  • Lunchbox and school-day planning
  • Gentle approaches for fussy eating
Book a Consultation
06

Therapeutic Diet Planning

Structured diet plans for specific health needs such as digestive, thyroid, kidney or heart-health concerns, planned in line with medical advice.

  • Clear foods-to-include and foods-to-limit guidance
  • Sample menus and recipe swaps
  • Regular review and plan refinement
Book a Consultation
